The feel

Easy to learn.Hard to stop.

The court is a quarter the size of tennis and the ball is slower, so beginners rally in minutes. Then the soft game takes over: dinks at the net, drops from the baseline and quick hands in the kitchen.

  • 01Serve underhand and diagonally; the ball must bounce once on each side before anyone volleys (the two-bounce rule).
  • 02No volleys inside the kitchen, the 7-ft zone either side of the net.
  • 03Games go to 11, win by 2. Most people play doubles.

What is pickleball?

Pickleball is a paddle sport that mixes tennis, badminton and table tennis. It's played with solid paddles and a light perforated plastic ball on a badminton-sized court, usually as doubles.

How much does pickleball cost at Go Play?

Pickleball memberships start from ₹6,000 a month. Coaching starts from ₹2,500 a month, in batches of up to six players.

Is pickleball good for beginners?

Yes. The underhand serve, small court and slow ball make it one of the easiest racket sports to start. Most beginners are rallying within their first session.

When can I book a pickleball court?

Court bookings run from 5 AM to 1 AM. Book on WhatsApp or by calling +91 92566 71777, or through the Go Play app.
