What sports can I play at Go Play Kota?

Pickleball, badminton, swimming, box cricket and football on the rooftop turf, table tennis and a full gym. E-sports & sim racing, group classes, Aqua Pilates and a Kids Gym are coming soon.

What are Go Play's timings?

The club is open every day from 6 AM to 12 AM. Courts can be booked from 5 AM to 1 AM. The pool and gym close at 11 PM.

How much does Go Play cost?

Courts by the hour: badminton and pickleball from ₹350 (11 AM–3 PM; ₹500 mornings, ₹500–₹600 evenings), turf from ₹1,000 and the pool ₹250 per swimmer, GST included. Memberships: badminton ₹1,200/month (yearly), pickleball ₹6,000/month, swimming ₹1,500/month (yearly), gym ₹1,125/month (yearly), gym + swim ₹1,750/month (yearly). The All-Pass starts from ₹2,083/month on a yearly plan.

What is the cheapest time to book a court at Go Play?

11 AM to 3 PM (happy hours). Pickleball and badminton courts cost ₹350 an hour then, against ₹500 in the morning and ₹500 (badminton) or ₹600 (pickleball) in the evening. Turf is ₹1,000 an hour at happy hours and ₹1,200 otherwise. Prices include GST and are the same on weekends.

How do I book a court?

Message or call +91 92566 71777 and the team will book for you, or use the Go Play app on Android and iPhone. Online booking on the website is opening soon.

Is there coaching for kids?

Yes. Badminton, pickleball, cricket and swimming coaching run in small batches for kids and adults. Batch timings are shared on request.

Where is Go Play located?

Go Play Sports Club & Academy is at H1-203 (C1), Rd. No. 5, Readymade Garment Zone, IPIA, Kota, Rajasthan 324005.

Can I take a tour before joining?

Yes. Book a club tour on WhatsApp or by phone. The tour covers the courts, pool, gym and café.
