Where is Go Play in Kota?

Go Play Sports Club & Academy is at H1-203 (C1), Rd. No. 5, Readymade Garment Zone, IPIA, Kota, Rajasthan 324005, in the Readymade Garment Zone of IPIA.

What are the opening hours?

Open every day, 6 AM to 12 AM. Courts can be booked 5 AM to 1 AM. Pool and gym close at 11 PM.

How do I book?

WhatsApp or call +91 92566 71777, or use the Go Play app on Android and iPhone. Online booking is opening soon.

Can I visit before joining?

Yes. Club tours cover the courts, pool, gym and café. Book one on WhatsApp or by phone.
