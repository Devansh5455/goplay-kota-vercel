Ask on WhatsAppAsk on WhatsApp
What does the All-Pass include?

One All-Pass covers courts, turf, pool, gym and group classes during your booked slot, with one card. Steam and cold shower are included.

How much is the All-Pass?

The All-Pass starts from ₹2,083 a month on a yearly plan.

Are there family or couple plans?

Yes. Individual, partner and family passes are available, and the team will advise on the best fit.

Can I try before joining?

Yes. Book a single court hour or a club tour on WhatsApp first.

How do I join?

Message +91 92566 71777 on WhatsApp, call, or use the Go Play app on Android and iPhone.
